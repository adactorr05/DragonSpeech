Dragon Speech

Grimoire of the Old Tongue

247 words

Every word the working understands, grouped by domain and laid out as crude → precise chains, since precision - not sentence length - is what actually decides a spell's cost.

How to read a chain: villa → blekkja → gervimynd - the same idea (confuse/deceive a mind), spoken with less and less guesswork. The indented, arrow-prefixed word requires the one above it. A crude word costs more to speak because the working has to assume the worst about what you meant; a precise one leaves nothing to guess.

Risk pill = how dangerous a wrong guess at this word is (hidden in-game until discovered). Bar = precision, 0-1. Chips = what the word actually does (effect handler, wards, summons, etc.) - if a verb has no effect chip, it isn't castable as a spell on its own (e.g. the five base Mind words, which instead unlock permanent duel skills).
