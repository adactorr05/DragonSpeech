ILLUSION MAGIC — DESIGN NOTES (NOT YET BUILT)
================================================

Status: not implemented. This is a design doc to pick up in a later
session, written from a real back-and-forth that settled every major
architectural question. Everything under "CONFIRMED DESIGN" below is
locked in; everything under "OPEN QUESTIONS / MY OWN ASSUMPTIONS" still
needs a decision before or during implementation.

Goes in: docs/ILLUSION_MAGIC.txt (repo docs folder, same place as
ANCIENT_LANGUAGE_STYLE_GUIDE.md and MIND_DUEL_PHASE6.md).


THE CORE IDEA
-------------

An illusion barrier hides a region of the world by moving it somewhere
else entirely, rather than trying to fake its appearance in place.

The original approach discussed was "make the blocks inside literally
invisible, so it looks like normal terrain from outside" — this was
explicitly rejected as too hard to do well (it needs intercepting real
chunk rendering, one of the genuinely hardest things to do cleanly in a
Fabric mod, and gets worse the moment you ask what a half-detected
player should see, or what a flickering torch inside the barrier looks
like from outside).

The approach settled on instead: when an illusion barrier is cast, every
chunk inside its volume is COPIED into a linked pocket dimension, at the
SAME coordinates the barrier occupies in its source dimension. The
original chunks stay physically in place in the source dimension the
whole time the illusion is up, but are hidden behind a solid/hazy
barrier shell (visually similar to the existing blue MagicBarrierEntity,
not a fake-terrain render) so nobody can just walk in or see through it
by accident. The pocket-dimension copy is what players actually
interact with once they're let through - it can diverge freely from the
original (built on, fought in, looted) while the illusion is up.


THE THREE VOID DIMENSIONS
--------------------------

Not one pocket dimension shared by everything - three:

- one mirroring the Overworld
- one mirroring the Nether
- one mirroring the End

Each mirror dimension is the same coordinate size as the real dimension
it links to. When a barrier is cast at a given set of coordinates, its
copied chunks land at those SAME coordinates in the matching void
mirror. Since two real illusion barriers can never occupy the same
overworld/nether/end coordinates in the first place (they'd collide in
the source dimension long before either could be cast), this guarantees
two illusions can never accidentally merge or overlap in the mirror
either - coordinate identity is the whole safety mechanism, no separate
allocation/bookkeeping needed.

Any number of illusions can coexist in the same mirror dimension this
way, entirely separated by their own real-world coordinates.

The mirror dimensions themselves are void - nothing generates there on
its own; they only ever contain what's been copied in by a barrier.


ENTERING AND LEAVING
---------------------

- You cannot walk through an illusion barrier by default - it blocks
  passage like the existing defensive barriers.
- Casting a "pass through" spell while near the barrier grants passage.
  Once cast, walking into the barrier's boundary teleports you into the
  linked pocket-dimension copy, arriving at the matching coordinates.
- To leave, you simply walk back out through the barrier boundary from
  inside the pocket dimension - this sends you back to the original
  (source) dimension at the matching coordinates.
- This is why the copied volume includes one buffer chunk around the
  barrier's own footprint in addition to the barrier's interior - the
  buffer is the actual walkable boundary zone where the "step back
  through" transition happens, not just padding.


GROWING AND SHRINKING
-----------------------

The barrier's size is tied to its own footprint, the same way the
existing defensive barriers can be grown/shrunk. For an illusion
barrier specifically:

- Growing it copies the newly-included chunks (plus a new buffer ring)
  into the mirror dimension, extending the hidden volume.
- Shrinking it either deletes the chunks that fall outside the new
  footprint, or keeps them as extra buffer space - either is acceptable;
  no strong preference expressed, implementer's call.


ENTITIES
--------

- Any entity present in the volume at the MOMENT the illusion is cast -
  players, mobs, animals, anything - gets pulled into the pocket
  dimension along with the copied terrain. It is not terrain-only; a
  Shade or a cow standing in the footprint when you cast this comes
  with it.
- Anyone who walks in afterward needs the "pass through" spell like
  normal - the initial pull-in only applies to whoever/whatever was
  already standing there at cast time.


DISPELLING
----------

To dispel an illusion barrier, you speak the SAME illusion word(s) used
to raise it, PLUS the general dispel word (plus whatever scopes/
modifiers apply) - not a separate bespoke "undo" word. This mirrors how
wards are already lowered (control + binding word together releases
your own ward of that type).

On dispel:
- Everything currently inside that barrier's pocket-dimension copy -
  terrain AND entities - is copied back to the source dimension,
  REPLACING whatever is there now (i.e. the original hidden terrain is
  overwritten by whatever the pocket copy had become, not restored to
  its pre-illusion state - the pocket copy is authoritative once the
  barrier's been up for any length of time).
- Any player (or entity) still physically inside the pocket dimension at
  the moment of dispel is pulled out and returned to the source
  dimension at the matching coordinates, same as walking out normally
  would have done. Nobody is left stranded in an orphaned, unlinked
  space.


THE GENERAL DISPEL WORD
-------------------------

The dispel word introduced for illusions is NOT illusion-specific - it
should also work as a general "remove a working" word for:

- marks (both removing your own placed mark, and removing a mark
  someone else placed - on yourself or on something else - exact scope
  rules TBD when this is built)
- traps (whatever the current or future "trap" mechanic in this mod
  turns out to be - needs to be checked against at implementation time,
  not assumed)

This is a genuinely new grammar capability (a general-purpose "undo
another working" word), not just an illusion-specific mechanic, and
should be designed with that generality in mind from the start rather
than retrofitted onto illusions alone later.


DETECTION
---------

A detection spell, cast while near a hidden barrier, reveals it as a
hazy white barrier shell (visually distinct from both the normal
"nothing here" appearance and the eventual "pass through" state) for as
long as the detection effect lasts. Being near the barrier is a
requirement for detection to work at all - this is not a long-range
sense.


THE ELVEN CITY
---------------

A new structure, built to spawn already hidden behind one of these
illusion barriers - the barrier and its pocket-dimension link are part
of how the structure generates, not something a player casts on top of
an existing normal structure. Its own generation approach (hand-placed
template vs. proper vanilla worldgen structure) is still an open
question - see below.


NEW WORDS NEEDED
-----------------

At minimum, this needs new dictionary entries for:
- casting an illusion barrier (likely its own binding-family word,
  parallel to verja/eldverja/etc., given how closely this mirrors the
  existing ward-raising grammar)
- detecting a hidden illusion
- passing through an illusion (temporary permission to cross)
- the general dispel word (illusions + marks + traps)

None of these have been named yet - word creation (Old Norse-style
naming, correct precision/domain/discovery-method values, fitting the
existing "How sentences work" grammar patterns) should happen together
with implementation, not decided blind ahead of time.


OPEN QUESTIONS / MY OWN ASSUMPTIONS (not yet confirmed)
---------------------------------------------------------

These came up during design but were left for implementation time
rather than settled now:

- Does simulation continue inside a pocket dimension while nobody is
  present (do villagers/mobs keep acting, do crops grow, does redstone
  run), or does it effectively freeze the way an unloaded chunk would?
  Vanilla's own chunk-loading/ticking rules may answer this for free
  depending on how the mirror dimension's chunks get force-loaded (or
  don't) - needs checking against whatever version of the game this
  gets built against.
- Exact collision/detection mechanism for "you are standing at the
  barrier boundary, walking further should teleport you" - likely an
  entity-tick or movement-event check against the barrier's stored
  bounding box, similar in spirit to how the existing MagicBarrierEntity
  already blocks movement, but needs its own design for the "let
  through if recently cast pass-through" state (a temporary buff/flag
  on the player, presumably, checked at the crossing moment).
- Chunk copying itself (structure blocks, block entities with their own
  NBT, any block that stores per-block state) needs real engineering
  attention - this is a well-trodden category of Minecraft modding
  problem (structure-block-style copying) and far more tractable than
  the rejected fake-terrain-rendering approach, but still needs care,
  especially for anything with tile-entity data.
- Elven city generation approach (hand-authored NBT/structure template
  the mod ships with vs. a true vanilla worldgen structure that
  generates naturally in new chunks) - very different scope and was
  intentionally left open rather than decided during this design pass.
